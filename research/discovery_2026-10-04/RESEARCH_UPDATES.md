# Research revisions during the timed run

## Validation failure review

At approximately15 minutes into the research session, the main checkpoint held2,150 trials. Rejection counts overlapped:1,820 had profit factor no higher than1.12,1,523 lost net money,967 had fewer than250 trades and605 breached20% drawdown. These are validation diagnostics, not reserved-test results. The refinement worker responds to cost drag by testing slower entry intervals and wider ATR stops/reward targets, to inadequate trade counts by testing shorter holding windows and less restrictive confirmation, and to drawdown breaches by lowering risk. Each parameter change and its result is recorded in adaptive_journal.jsonl.

## Further primary-source research

[Hansen, Kim and Kimbrough, Periodicity in Cryptocurrency Volatility and Liquidity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.12142) finds systematic time-of-day/day-of-week volume and volatility patterns across BTC/ETH and several venues, associated with algorithmic activity and funding times. This supports studying session-conditioned entry opportunities. It does not show that a specific07:00–16:00 UTC breakout rule earns net profits. The session-breakout family's window remains a pre-test hypothesis; its later performance must stand on its own.

[Wen et al., Intraday Return Predictability: Momentum, Reversal, or Both](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4080253) reports regime-dependent intraday momentum/reversal in older data. Jumps, liquidity and macro announcement conditions change relationships. That supports separating trend and range rules rather than expecting one candle formation to work in every market. The sample ends in2020 and cannot establish a current edge. This lab does not have a verified historical announcement calendar, so it does not claim news-conditioned backtests.

[Arefev, Cross-Sectional Momentum: Net-of-Costs Replication, September2026 preprint](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7404139) presents a recent constrained replication with explicit trading costs and survivorship handling. Its permitted weekly momentum specifications fail significance after costs, with rebalancing phase sensitivity. Independent preprint evidence is not definitive, but its negative result is relevant: the relative-strength family here combines ranking with a causal timing trigger and must survive net costs; a gross momentum anomaly is not assumed sufficient.

[Liu et al., Liquidity Commonality in Cryptocurrencies](https://hull-repository.worktribe.com/output/5336140) documents shared liquidity and weekly patterns. This reinforces the need to treat simultaneous coin positions as correlated risk and to resample daily portfolio outcomes rather than individual trades as if independent. Its existence does not identify profitable entry hours for this venue.

[Plazuelo Pascual et al., Price Discovery in Cryptocurrency Markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08718) studies CEX/DEX and spot/futures leadership. It supports distinguishing venues and asynchronous prices; it does not validate cross-coin hourly BTC lead/lag. The lab therefore treats BTC relative drift as an explicitly separate hypothesis, not a direct replication of cross-venue lead/lag evidence. Cross-chain arbitrage remains outside this candle simulator because executable pool routes, delays and MEV are unavailable.

## Learnings recorded before final testing

The simple unfiltered ridge forecasts were near50% direction accuracy in validation. Cost-aware filtering improved a smaller selected subset but produced overlapping events. This evidence argues for restraint in claims about highly accurate AI and for evaluating executed trades separately. Historical funding data was collected for all14 markets to audit the fixed candidates independently of the continuous debit allowance. The final funding audit does not change entry rules after seeing final returns.

The main worker briefly stopped on a Windows atomic-file replacement conflict. It resumed from the checkpoint with retry handling. The refinement worker, market-depth recorder and research review continued through that interval. The original start time and checkpoint-resume count are retained in session.json; the report must not claim uninterrupted execution of the main worker.

An independent engine review found that positions exiting intrabar could briefly create an unrealised equity peak at the later candle close. The final audited engine values those positions at their actual simulated exit once the exit bar has completed. A regression check verifies that a target-filled position cannot acquire a phantom peak at a later higher close. Search-worker drawdown envelopes were more conservative; this correction does not select entry rules from final-period outcomes. The funding-audited report is the primary final result.

## New source-driven families added during the session

[Kitron and Wengrowicz, Short-horizon Mean Reversion, August2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21888) reports directional reversal on many15-minute crypto pairs, while its gross edge is only about1.3basis points against an assumed5bp round-trip cost. This is evidence of the gap between detectable direction and profitable execution, not a high-profit trading recipe. This lab adds a cost-filtered sign-reversal family with magnitude and range restrictions, using Bybit data and materially higher retail cost assumptions. It is an independent transfer test, not an exact replication of the paper's Binance sample or historical taker-flow conditioning.

[He, Manela, Ross and von Wachter, Fundamentals of Perpetual Futures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06888) derives pricing bounds with trading costs and investigates basis deviations. It motivates treating funding and price risk jointly; it does not establish a universal directional signal from the sign of funding. Two further hypotheses are tested: crowded funding combined with a reversal candle and range context, and directional volume breakouts after a known past settlement. These are explicitly speculative translations, not a delta-neutral arbitrage claim. Historical rates are delayed by a full hour before entering signal features, and the publication-delay/future-invariance check passes.

[Ackerer, Hugonnier and Jermann, Perpetual Futures Pricing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11771) explains the price-anchoring mechanism and replication under specified funding rules. The theory is plausible, but issuer funding design and no-arbitrage pricing are not evidence that these entry rules will survive fees. The supplementary loop selects only on validation and freezes before its final tests.

## Historical market-cap eligibility and primary freezing policy

The primary audit adds known weekly top100 entry eligibility from [dated CoinMarketCap snapshots](https://coinmarketcap.com/historical/).145 of146 requested snapshots were retrieved successfully. The assumed publication lag is two days; records older than ten days are rejected. Future snapshots cannot affect earlier eligibility, and the staleness/delay check passes. Relative-strength percentile features in the primary audit exclude ineligible observed coins. The14-market basket is still selected using available histories and current interest; this is not a survivorship-free complete market panel. The initial validation search used a wider fixed basket, and the predeclared primary audit adds the historical membership restriction without changing entry parameters on final results.

Before final-period results were inspected, primary per-family selection was strengthened to prioritise validation qualification, then sufficient trade count, then validation score. Below250 trades, count precedes score. primary_freezer.py reads only validation records and writes PRIMARY_FROZEN.json after the search closes. This prevents a one-trade, loss-free PF cap from displacing a sufficiently sampled candidate. Loss-free tiny samples are reported as having no estimable PF rather than a fabricated finite estimate.

The primary audit starts new positions at their actual fill price. Drawdown uses a conservative favourable-then-adverse OHLC envelope, with fixed targets and stops limiting marks. This is not tick-level drawdown; simultaneous market extrema need not occur together. Eight causal, funding, membership and accounting regression checks passed before the final audit.

## Final-window source critique

[Bui and Nguyen, AdaptiveTrend preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11708v1) motivates intermediate timeframes, trailing exits and monthly asset selection. Its reported results are not independently replicated here. A concrete inconsistency limits confidence: section4.2 models eight-hour funding, while section5.6 claims four funding events daily when arguing for six-hour bars. Four daily events imply six-hour spacing, not eight-hour spacing. Funding schedules vary by instrument and date, which is why this lab uses observed settlements. The paper also supplies no code link in the rendered text and does not settle historical universe/delist treatment sufficiently for this implementation. Retain the hypotheses, not the headline Sharpe as validated evidence. This review occurs after candidate freezing and changes no candidate settings.

[David Cox CMT/CFA, Fill the Gap65](https://cmtassociation.org/podcast/fill-the-gap-episode-sixty-five-the-technically-driven-portfolio-with-david-cox-cmt-cfa/) offers a credentialed practitioner's discussion of technical portfolio management. It supports research questions about structural trends and tactical risk reduction; an interview does not validate a specific crypto entry, timeframe, or fee-adjusted edge.
