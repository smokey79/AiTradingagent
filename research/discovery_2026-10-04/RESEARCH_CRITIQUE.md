# Sources and testable hypotheses — 4 October 2026

Research sources suggest hypotheses; none proves that this account can trade profitably. Existing local strategy results were reviewed before constructing this lab.

| Primary source | Assessment | Consequence for this lab |
|---|---|---|
| [Liu and Tsyvinski, Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24877) | Crypto-specific momentum and attention are relevant. The old, small asset sample and longer horizons cannot establish an hourly edge today. | Test trend persistence, relative strength and BTC lead/lag separately; require later-period evidence. |
| [Liu, Tsyvinski and Wu, Common Risk Factors in Cryptocurrency](https://www.nber.org/papers/w25882) | Cross-sectional market, size and momentum factors suggest shared exposures. Correlated coins are not independent experiments. | Use a shared collateral and exposure limit, cross-market breadth, and daily blocks in uncertainty checks. |
| [Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen, Time Series Momentum](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/Time-Series-Momentum) | Strong evidence concerns futures and much longer lookbacks. Applying it to the next few crypto candles is an extrapolation. | Higher-timeframe direction plus lower-timeframe entry is a hypothesis; tune lookback in bars and report elapsed hours. |
| [Lo, Mamaysky and Wang, Foundations of Technical Analysis](https://www.nber.org/papers/w7613) | Algorithmic patterns can contain information in historical equities. Information is not a profitable crypto trade after costs. | Define candle/pivot patterns precisely; count next-candle outcomes, contextualise by regime, and keep confirmed pivots causal. |
| [Cont, Kukanov and Stoikov, Price Impact of Order Book Events](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402) | Order-flow imbalance and depth have a plausible price-impact mechanism. Evidence is from equities; displayed volume can vanish. | Record actual book updates and completed trade flow separately. Do not infer historical order books from candle volume. |
| [Gould and Bonart, Queue Imbalance as a One-Tick-Ahead Price Predictor](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03492) | Very short-horizon imbalance prediction does not establish enough price movement to pay taker costs. Queue priority and latency matter. | Treat live imbalance as an execution/research adviser. A 90-minute sample cannot qualify a historical strategy. |
| [Bailey and López de Prado, Deflated Sharpe Ratio](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf) | Searching many variants makes the best sample result optimistic. Non-normality and selection must be disclosed. | Record every trial; freeze validation-selected rules before final tests. Base qualification is a screening threshold, not statistical proof. |
| [Bysik and Ślepaczuk, Bitcoin ML Trading Under Transaction Costs, 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00060v1) | The paper uses chronological folds and cost-aware thresholds. Architecture dominance and benchmark superiority are not established statistically; single-asset results may not transfer. It synthesises a small number of missing bars. | Test net economics rather than accuracy alone, compare doubled fees/slippage, never fabricate missing price bars, and keep cost-sensitive entry selection as a follow-up hypothesis. |
| [Hyndman and Athanasopoulos, free Forecasting: Principles and Practice, §5.10](https://otexts.com/fpp3/tscv.html) | Forecasts must be evaluated using only earlier training observations. Multi-step horizons need their own evaluation. This is a forecasting textbook, not a trading recipe. | Fixed chronological train/validation/test split, causal feature-prefix tests, and separate 1/2/3-candle prediction outcomes. |
| [CMT Association, Fill the Gap episode 62, Keith Lerner CMT/CFA](https://cmtassociation.org/podcast/fill-the-gap-episode-sixty-two-weight-of-the-evidence-with-keith-lerner-cmt-cfa/) | Credentialed practitioners describe weighing evidence and adjusting to changing markets. Interview experience is not a controlled profitability study. | Track trend/range regimes and conflicting signals; do not switch strategies after one loss. |
| [CMT Association, Fill the Gap episode 23](https://cmtassociation.org/podcast/fill-the-gap-episode-twenty-three-live-at-apac-summit/) | Credentialed hosts discuss trend engagement and money management. | [Crypto Wash Trading](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10984) | Exchange volume can be misleading; the study does not label every current Bybit print as manipulation. | Volume is a filter, not independent proof of demand; inspect liquidity and completed trade flow. |

## What changed from earlier attempts

The September BTC strategy lab already tried classic Donchian, SuperTrend, ADX, VWAP reversion, liquidity sweeps and RSI divergence. Its surviving ETH EMA/VWAP result had only 238 full-period trades, below the current 250-trade requirement. The September multi-timeframe lab used looser thresholds. Neither is adopted as a current winner.

This lab adds confirmed-pivot anchored VWAP, delayed-confirmation trendline breaks, compression plus higher-timeframe alignment, failed-auction range reclaims, engulfing/inside/three-bar triggers, cross-sectional strength, BTC relative drift, OBV/price disagreement, efficiency and volatility regime filters. A plain EMA/VWAP family is labelled a comparison baseline. Families that overlap old concepts change regime, execution and risk rules; overlap is disclosed rather than presented as wholly new research.

The saved Prodjects archive contains 21 documents. The review records function names and concepts without copying credentials. It includes multichain arbitrage and gas optimisation, but does not establish validated backtest performance. Cross-chain arbitrage is excluded from directional candle simulation: bridge time, pool routes, inventory, MEV, gas and executable bid/ask prices require another dataset.

## Realism limits

The final period is excluded from parameter selection in this run. Earlier local studies had already used parts of the2026 history, and their findings informed this research. Therefore this is not a globally untouched historical holdout across the entire research programme. A new forward paper-trading sample is still required before deployment.

Only current top-100 members with available cached and refreshed Bybit histories are used; the full top 100 is not claimed. This introduces survivorship bias. Current order filters are applied throughout; historical filter changes are not reconstructed. Funding uses an explicitly conservative debit allowance, not the actual historical signed funding series. Intrabar ordering is unavailable: stops take precedence when both stop and target are touched. The drawdown envelope assumes simultaneous adverse extremes across markets. Historical book depth, execution latency, pool routing and MEV are not reconstructed. Gas stress is a scenario, not a validated DEX backtest.

The account is fully collateralised, with no leverage, 0.5% equity risk per trade, at most three positions, 25% per-market notional and 60% aggregate exposure. Stops and gaps can exceed planned risk. Trades last at most five days, consistent with the unrecovered-allocation gate. Qualification requires positive final-test net profit, at least 250 trades, both dollar and notional-return profit factors above 1.12, and drawdown no higher than 20%.
